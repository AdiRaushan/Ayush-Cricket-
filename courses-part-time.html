PROGRAM POLICIES

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Standard guidelines governing part-time practice and short-stay residential boarding.

Daily Rate & Minimum Stay

Residential Practice is ₹2,500 per day with a minimum reservation of 10 consecutive days (minimum total ₹25,000). Not convertible into prorated discounts.

Boarding & Nutrition Inclusions

Includes dormitory shared air-cooled rooms with attached washrooms, daily laundry service for cricket kits, and 4 fresh vegetarian meals per day.

Match Practice Sessions

Up to 50 structured practice and match sessions annually reflects full-calendar training availability and is subject to weather and scheduling.

Trial Evaluation Sessions

One-day trial slots and applicable fees for the part-time format must be confirmed directly with academy administration prior to arrival.

Operational Availability & Scheduling

Program availability, training schedules, batch allotments, and dormitory room vacancies are subject to academy confirmation. Practice operates Tuesday to Sunday with Mondays reserved for athletic rest.

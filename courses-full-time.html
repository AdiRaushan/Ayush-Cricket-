FULL-TIME PROGRAM FAQ

Direct, factual answers regarding schedules, fees, accommodation, and academy infrastructure.

The full-time cricket programs operate 6 days a week, from Tuesday to Sunday. Mondays are designated rest and recovery days.
Full-day programs run two sessions: Morning session from 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM (skills & drills) and Evening session from 3:30 PM – 6:30 PM (match simulations & conditioning). Evening full-time program runs from 3:30 PM – 6:30 PM.
For non-residential, the ₹17,700/mo fee includes coaching, 80+ matches with match costs, gym, video analysis, bowling machine, lunch, and snacks. Residential fees include boarding, 4 daily meals, laundry, and 100+ matches. All fees include GST.
Yes, residential programs offer 8, 6, 4, 3, or 2 student room-sharing. Facilities include air-cooled rooms, attached washrooms, 4 daily meals, laundry, and 24-hour security supervision.
Yes, we offer a Full-Time Residential Program With Schooling. Regular academic school curriculum is integrated with academy training. The annual school fee is ₹50,000* (excluding admission & miscellaneous fees).
Rooms are offered in 8-sharing (₹35,700/mo), 6-sharing (₹39,700/mo), 4-sharing (₹44,900/mo), 3-sharing (₹51,200/mo), and 2-sharing (₹65,000/mo). All fees include GST.
Non-residential trials are ₹1,000 for a 1-day session. Residential trials are ₹5,000 for 2 days with all boarding and meals included.
Yes, match costs for scheduled academy fixtures (minimum 80 annually for non-residential, 100+ for residential) are included in the program fee.
2 full-size match grounds with turf wickets, all-weather indoor/outdoor nets (turf, astro, cemented), automated bowling machine, 4K video analysis lab, and in-house fitness gym.
All coaching programs are led by BCCI and NIS certified coaching staff with extensive professional cricket and competitive mentorship experience.

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Important guidelines regarding fee structure, admissions, and facility usage.

Fees & GST Applicability

All displayed fee amounts are inclusive of applicable GST unless explicitly noted. Fees are payable in advance on a monthly or specified term basis.

One-Time Admission Fees

₹50,000 for non-residential; ₹70,000 for residential (₹50k academy + ₹20k hostel); ₹120,000 for residential with schooling (includes ₹50,000 school fee deposit*).

Trials & Evaluations

One-day non-residential trial: ₹1,000. Two-day residential trial with meals and boarding: ₹5,000. Prior registration required before attending.

Boarding & Nutrition

Room allotment depends on selected configuration (8, 6, 4, 3, or 2 sharing). Meals in dining hall are strictly vegetarian, balanced for athletic nutrition.

Schooling Program

Applies strictly to candidates under "With Schooling". Stated annual school fee of ₹50,000* excludes uniform, admission, and board exam misc charges.

Matches & Availability

Stated 80–100+ match counts represent scheduled tournament and intra-academy fixtures. Batch allocations subject to academy confirmation.
